Exported JSON includes canvas size, optional embedded image data, and the full list of anchor + handle positions.

How to use

Click on the canvas to add anchors. Use Adjust handles to drag anchor points or their in/out handles. Select Remove point and click a point to delete it.

Saved curves can be loaded with loadCurveData from reader.js and sampled with evaluateCurve(t) (where t is 0-1).

Click on the canvas to edit. Use Shift + drag to keep handles mirrored.
